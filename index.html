MISMO Link AI Beta

Beta Agreement

Two agreements, both Version 1.0 (updated 07/01/2026): the company signs the SaaS Subscriber Agreement once, then each tester signs the User SaaS Agreement for themselves.

Subscriber Agreement — the Company

Sent to each beta company's contact. They, or whoever signs for the company, read the agreement, type their name and sign. A copy comes back to them by email, and the signature is recorded in Who Has Signed.

Open the Signing Page

User Agreement — Each Tester

Once the company has signed, the company passes this link to each person taking part in the beta. Each one reads the agreement and signs for themselves, and the signature is recorded the same way.

Open the Signing Page

Who Is Told When Someone Signs

When a company signs, these people are emailed to schedule the kick-off call and send the company the User Agreement link. When a tester signs, they are emailed to set that person up. Each signer also gets their own copy.

What Gets Recorded

Which agreement was signed; the company, and the signer's name, job title and work email; the date and time; the version of the agreement; the exact sentence they agreed to; a fingerprint of the agreement text as it stood on screen; and the address they signed from.
