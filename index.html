Beta Agreement

Two agreements, both Version 1.0 (updated 07/01/2026). Each beta company signs the SaaS Subscriber Agreement once, through someone authorized to sign for it. Each person who will use MISMO Link AI then signs the User SaaS Agreement for themselves.

Subscriber Agreement — the Company

Send this link to the beta company's contact. They, or whoever signs for the company, read the agreement, type their name and sign. A copy comes back to them by email, and the signature is recorded automatically.

Open the Signing Page

User Agreement — Each Tester

Send this link to each person at the company who will use MISMO Link AI. They read the agreement, type their name and sign for themselves. A copy comes back to them by email, and the signature is recorded the same way as the company's.

Open the Signing Page

Who Is Told When Someone Signs

When a company signs the Subscriber Agreement, these people get an email to schedule the kick-off call and send the company the User Agreement link. When a tester signs the User Agreement, they get an email to set that person up. Each signer also gets their own copy.
